## Brand & Style

This design system translates the thoughtful, deliberate tactile experience of physical stationery, laboratory field notes, and archival journals into an interactive personal learning roadmap tracker. The interface addresses self-directed learners, technical researchers, and knowledge workers who seek an antidote to hyper-sterilized, corporate software dashboards. 

The emotional signature is grounded, studious, and tangibly earned:
- **Tactile Materiality:** Rather than synthetic glassmorphism or sterile flat minimalism, surfaces evoke genuine physical media—uncoated heavy paper stock, deckled edges, subtle dot-matrix drafting guides, and subtle paper grain.
- **Archival Precision:** Typographic and line structures take inspiration from technical logbooks, field guides, and annotated manuscripts. Progress is rendered as authentic physical markings: fountain pen lines, faint pencil annotations, felt-tip highlighter sweeps, and inked postal stamps.
- **Intentional Restraint:** Visual delight comes not from bouncy app animations, but from crisp geometric drafting lines, understated binder-clip tabs, washi-tape labels, and the warmth of faded kraft stock.

The design movement combines **Tactile Stationery Realism** with **Modern Editorial Utility**, ensuring information density and roadmap readability remain primary while celebrating the ritual of physical record-keeping.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic profile of physical archival stationery, formulated with lightfast pigments and non-reflective finishes.

### Surface & Canvas Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Unbleached warm cotton rag paper. Used as the global application background and unselected roadmap canvassing.
- **Sheet Stock (`#F4EFE6`):** Natural ivory ledger sheet. Used for cards, elevated surfaces, and modular roadmap nodes.
- **Kraft Sepia (`#E2D9CC` / `#D5C7B2`):** Manila folder and heavy kraft envelope tones. Used for structural dividers, inactive node borders, and subtle structural frames.

### Ink & Drafting Tones
- **Archival Ink Primary (`#1E232A`):** Deep carbon charcoal with a hint of dark gall ink. Serves as the primary typographic color and crisp boundary stroke.
- **Pencil Graphite (`#5A646E`):** Mid-tone 2B graphite grey for secondary descriptions, metadata, and structural node connector paths.
- **Faint Drafting Grey (`#A8A29E`):** Thin ruled-line grey for dot grids, crosshairs, and millimeter margin markers.

### Stationery Accents & Annotations
- **Terracotta Wax Stamp (`#C86446` / Light Tint `#FCEBE6`):** Secondary accent. Evokes crimson sealing wax and archival rubber stamps. Used for critical milestones, active markers, and warning notices.
- **Botanical Sage (`#2E5C52` / Mint Highlighter Tint `#E6F5EE`):** Tertiary accent. Evokes botanical field ink and green highlighter pen. Denotes "Completed" roadmap steps and verified mastery.
- **Fluorescent Ochre Highlighter (`#F4CE42` / Soft Tint `#FEF9C3`):** High-visibility felt-tip marker wash. Applied with semi-transparent multiply blending to signify "In Progress", active focus tracks, and bookmark tabs.
- **Drafting Cyan (`#3B82F6` / Soft Tint `#EFF6FF`):** Blueprint ink used sparingly for reference external links and technical documentation citations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary grace with technical logbook documentation.

1. **The Literary Editorial Voice (`Newsreader`):** Used for roadmap titles, topic headings, and editorial narrative. Features generous proportions, optical sizing, and organic serifs that recall pressed paper books and scientific journals. Headlines should use italic styles sparingly to denote annotated subtitles or learner thoughts.
2. **The Functional Legibility Engine (`Work Sans`):** A modern, geometric grotesque with warm grotesque proportions. Assigned to broad descriptive paragraphs, curriculum descriptions, resource notes, and interactive modal copy. Ensures high legibility against tinted paper backgrounds.
3. **The Index & Log Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Reflects mechanical typewriter markings, stamp dates, file reference codes, status flags, and node coordinates (e.g., `[SEC-04 // NODE-09]`). All monospace labels default to uppercase with tracking applied to mimic mechanical carriage-return spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure takes cues from drafting paper and field journal notebooks, using an underlying **8px baseline module** aligned with a visible or implicit **24px dot-grid canvas** (e.g., `background-size: 24px 24px`).

### Layout Philosophy
- **Field Canvas Architecture:** Roadmaps are organized within an infinite or expansive canvas with visible ruled borders and left-hand red margin rules (referencing classic notebook margins positioned `72px` from the sheet edge).
- **Cartographic Visual Density:** Connecting tracks follow orthogonal right-angle pathways (reminiscent of circuit diagrams or technical subway charts) spaced cleanly using `gutter` tokens.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column exploratory canvas. Nodes align to 24px incremental grid lines. Left sidebar functions as a fixed index tab reminiscent of a tabbed binder.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column format. Side margins reduce to `2rem`. Roadmap branching converts to vertical cascade streams with lateral offset indents.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Linear vertical progression spine. The tree unfolds as a continuous timeline along an inked left-hand vertical ruler line. Margins pull tight to `1.25rem` to maximize legible reading area.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids diffuse algorithmic drop shadows and digital floating blurs. Instead, depth is conveyed through **paper stratification, tactile offsets, and micro-press impressions**:

### 1. Paper Tiers (Physical Layering)
- **Layer 0 (Desk Surface / Background):** The foundational parchment `#FAF8F5` overlaid with a subtle 1px dot grid pattern (`radial-gradient(#D5C7B2 1px, transparent 1px)` at 24px intervals).
- **Layer 1 (Notebook Sheet):** Cards and panels rendered in `#F4EFE6` with crisp 1px borders in `#D5C7B2`.
- **Layer 2 (Overlaid Index Cards / Modals):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` or stark `#FAF8F5` stock sitting atop the baseline sheet.

### 2. Tactile Block Cast Shadows
Elevation is expressed using physical hard-edge offset shadows that emulate thick cardboard or paper swatches:
- **Resting Node / Card:** `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px 0px #D5C7B2;`
- **Active / Focused Node:** `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #1E232A;`
- **Interactive Hover:** Physical translation upward by `-2px` on both axes, matched by an expanding hard shadow: `transform: translate(-2px, -2px); box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px 0px #1E232A;`
- **Pressed State:** `transform: translate(2px, 2px); box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px 0px #1E232A;`

### 3. Debossed & Stamped Insets
- Inner search bars, code blocks, and completed checklist tracks utilize a subtle debossed "pressed-paper" effect: `box-shadow: inset 1px 1px 2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.08);` with a solid 1px bottom border highlight in `#FFFFFF`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary replicates precisely guillotine-cut paper, clipped stationery cards, and adhesive tape strips:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to roadmap nodes, cards, buttons, and popovers. Elements retain the sturdy, clean-cut feel of cardstock rather than digital "bubble" UI.
- **Deckled / Punched Edges:** Specialized containers (e.g., node detail trays or coupon-style reward milestones) may incorporate 45-degree corner notches (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) or perforated dashed outlines (`border-style: dashed`).
- **Washi Strips & Sticky Flags:** Status chips and category markers use asymmetrical rectangular cuts, occasionally with one notched edge resembling torn paper tape.

## Components

### 1. Roadmap Node (Core Component)
- **Anatomy:** Compact cardstock module containing a step coordinate (e.g., `01.2`), title, time-estimate label, and status indicator.
- **Default State:** Ivory sheet (`#F4EFE6`), 1.5px solid border in `#D5C7B2`, 2px hard offset shadow.
- **In-Progress State:** A top-edge horizontal washi tape strip in Highlighter Yellow (`#F4CE42`), black border (`#1E232A`), active pulsing pencil indicator.
- **Completed State:** Botanical Sage border (`#2E5C52`), subtle diagonal ruled background hatch, and an organic diagonal rubber stamp reading `DONE` or `VERIFIED` in faded green ink at a -4deg angle.
- **Connector Lines:** Solid 2px lines in `#1E232A` for unlocked paths; 2px dashed strokes in `#A8A29E` for locked/upcoming pathways.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Fountain Pen Inked):** Solid `#1E232A` background with `#FAF8F5` text, styled in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. Border: 1.5px solid `#1E232A`. Crisp hard shadow `3px 3px 0px #C86446`.
- **Secondary Action (Cardstock Blank):** Background `#F4EFE6`, text `#1E232A`, border `1.5px solid #1E232A`. Hover triggers a Highlighter Yellow wash tint (`#FEF9C3`).
- **Annotation Button (Ghost / Handwritten):** Transparent fill, underlined with a wavy or double-ruled border; text styles set in italicized `Newsreader`.

### 3. Checkboxes & Progress Markers
- Replaces generic circles with square brass wire binders or pencil review boxes (`18px x 18px`).
- **Checked State:** A hand-drawn charcoal graphite checkmark (`✓`) or an inked diagonal cross (`✕`) rendered inside a warm white square with an authentic 1.5px graphite stroke.

### 4. Chips, Tags & Washi Tape Flags
- **Monospace Category Chip:** High-contrast `JetBrains Mono` labels wrapped in a pale kraft boundary (`#E2D9CC`) with a leading hash or bracket (`[FRONTEND]`).
- **Washi Tape Milestone Tag:** Semi-transparent pastel strip with simulated ripped lateral edges, layered over cards with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` and a subtle 1-degree rotation (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)`).

### 5. Input Fields
- Resembles ruled ledger paper lines: background transparent `#FAF8F5`, resting state stripped of vertical side borders, featuring only a solid 1.5px bottom baseline stroke in `#D5C7B2`.
- When focused, the baseline turns solid `#1E232A` and reveals a faint ivory highlight fill with a typewriter block caret (`▋`).

### 6. Cards & Detail Drawers (Field Notebook Folios)
- Cards feature simulated two-hole binder punches or brass paperclips fixed to the header.
- Modals slide in like an indexed field folder, complete with a stepped Manila tab header denoting the topic hierarchy.
